# Myia's Year of the Horse

An interactive museum-wall timeline made for the i.c.stars Cycle 60 **Year of the Horse** leadership workshop.

The wall is told backward in time. It starts in 2054 and walks back to 2025 to show what got me there. I wrote it as a declaration of the future I'm willing to grow into.

## The 10 stops

| Timeframe | Years |
|---|---|
| Past | 2025 |
| Present | 2026 |
| Near future | 2027, 2028, 2029 |
| Long-range vision | 2034, 2039, 2044, 2049, 2054 |

Each year has a title, a fortune, images, a reflection, a favorite dish and a soundtrack song.

## How to view it

- **Start the tour** gives each year 30 seconds. The red arrow on the rail moves like a song's progress bar, and photos pop up one after another.
- Use the arrow buttons or your keyboard's left and right arrow keys to move between years.
- Tap any photo to see it up close, and tap **Read the full reflection** for the whole story.
- Each year's song plays through a Spotify player in the audio guide bar.

## Run it

It's a single self-contained `index.html` file. Open it in any browser, or view it on GitHub Pages.
